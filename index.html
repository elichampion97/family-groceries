Family guide

How everything works

Build the list

Add saved items, create something new, scan a barcode, or choose recipe ingredients. Confirm quantity, store, and estimate before it reaches Needed.

Shop and verify

Filter Needed by store, start Trip mode, check off purchases, then scan the receipt to verify prices and add unplanned purchases to History.

Gemini assistance

Gemini reads receipts and recipes, identifies package photos, prepares the shared weekly review, plans meals, and answers questions. It never changes family data without confirmation.

Recipes and leftovers

Recipe originals are stored in Family Drive. Meal planning can reuse saved recipes and available leftovers, then send only selected missing ingredients to Needed.

Privacy

The webpage contains no Gemini key or private family data. The key stays in Apps Script, lists and history stay in the private Sheet, and recipe originals stay in Family Drive.
